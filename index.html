About Chileka Youth Organization

Established in 2025, Chileka Youth Organization is a non-profit dedicated to securing rights of the poor and marginalized, promoting good governance, and developing sustainable livelihoods in Malawi.

Our main thrust is promoting people's participation at all levels of the state and society, accountability, capacity development and access to resources.

Our Vision

"A society where children live happily in a safe, caring and stimulating environment that promotes the best of their potential towards a poverty free society."

Our Mission

"To uphold human dignity, equity and accountability through empowering and protecting vulnerable children and their communities so that these children can lead a bright future."
